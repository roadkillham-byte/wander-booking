Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026. Written in plain words; Wander Coffee Van (ABN 62 731 017 112) is the only one who sees this information.

What we collect

When you send us a booking request we keep the details you give us: your name, phone, email, event date and number of guests. That is how we send your quote and call you.

We also record how our pages are used, so we can make booking easier. We note things like:

  • which page you opened, and the website or link that brought you here
  • the people and hours you tried in the price calculator, and whether you looked at the 4-Pack
  • how far down the page you went and roughly how long you stayed
  • whether you are on a phone or a computer, your language, your time zone and the hour of day
  • which links in our emails you tapped, and any one-tap answers you gave us (for example, what kind of team you are)

We do not record what you type, your location beyond your time zone, or anything about other websites you visit. We do not use advertising or tracking companies.

How it is linked to you

Your browser keeps a random number so we can tell a repeat visit from a new one. It is not your name. If you send a request or tap a link in our email, we may connect that number to your request so we can look after you better.

Why

To give you a quote, to book and run your morning, to follow up once if you have not replied, and to improve our prices, our pages and our emails.

Who sees it, and where it is kept

Only Wander. We never sell it or give it to anyone to market to you. It is stored in our Google account and in our own business software. Google stores data in other countries, including the United States.

Your choices

  • If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we record nothing about your visit.
  • Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To see what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email wandermcco@gmail.com and we will do it.
